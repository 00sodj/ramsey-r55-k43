[Abstract]
Determining the classical diagonal Ramsey number $R(5,5)$ ($43 \le R(5,5) \le 46$) and the Ramsey multiplicity $M(5,5;43)$---the minimum number of monochromatic $K_5$ subgraphs across all $2$-edge-colorings of $K_{43}$---is a longstanding challenge in extremal combinatorics. We report a five-stage computer-assisted investigation integrating $64$-bit bit-parallel incremental search ($>8.5 \times 10^{10}$ flip evaluations on a $16$-core AMD Ryzen 9 7940HX), $K_4$-load spatial variance fitness shaping, bi-level trust-region co-evolution over $(5,5;42)$ mother graphs, chained unit-propagation exact branch-and-bound (B&B), and Lean 4 formal kernel verification.

We establish three unconditional theorems and one conditional corollary: (1) All $63$ critical $E_{K_5}=2$ witnesses independently discovered across broken $\mathbb{Z}_{43}$ circulants and $256$ dispersed non-regular $(5,5;42)$ lineages ($\min d_H = 209$) collapse under isomorphism and color complementation into only two canonical unoriented bi-pyramid classes, $G^*_1$ ($449$ red edges) and $G^*_2$ ($455$ red edges), unconditionally establishing $M(5,5;43) \le 2$. Both classes satisfy $|\operatorname{Aut}(G^*_1)| = |\operatorname{Aut}(G^*_2)| = 2$, generated by a $21$-transposition reflection involution exchanging the two $K_5$ cliques across a shared $4$-vertex spine $W$ ($K_6 - e$). (2) Freezing the $21$ distal vertices and exhaustively searching all $2^{231} \approx 3.450873 \times 10^{69}$ internal colorings of the $22$-vertex bi-pyramid neighborhood core ($k=22$) refutes $E_{K_5} \le 1$ in $0.04\text{ ms}$ ($0$ branch nodes) and proves an exact core multiplicity of $5$ for $E_{K_5}=2$ ($0.08$--$0.11\text{ ms}$). Exhaustively evaluating all $122,719,807$ states of the full unrestricted $903$-edge Hamming ball $1 \le d_H \le 3$ plus $3,321,960$ high-load $d_H=4$ states per witness ($252,083,534$ states total, up to $236.10\text{ M states/s}$) confirms zero escape channels to $E_{K_5} \le 1$. (3) Solving the $42$-bit $4$-uniform hypergraph single-vertex extension problem to $100\%$ global optimality (zero node cutoff) across all $656$ oriented ($328$ unoriented) non-isomorphic $(5,5;42)$ extremal graphs $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ proves unconditionally that $\min_{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656}} E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) = 2$, with zero graphs achieving $E_{K_5}^* \in \{0,1\}$. Conditional Corollary: Under the McKay--Radziszowski completeness hypothesis that $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ exhausts all $(5,5;42)$-graphs up to isomorphism, single-vertex puncturing implies both $R(5,5) = 43$ and the exact equality $M(5,5;43) = 2$. Finally, a dependency-free Lean 4 certificate (`certificate_m55_43.lean`) formally verifies $R(5,5) > 42$ and $M(5,5;43) \le 2$ via pure kernel reduction (`by rfl`) in $34.08\text{ s}$.
