[Abstract]
Determining the diagonal Ramsey number $R(5,5)$ ($43 \le R(5,5) \le 46$) and the Ramsey multiplicity $M(5,5;43)$---the minimum number of monochromatic $K_5$ subgraphs over all $2$-edge-colorings of $K_{43}$---remains open. We present a five-stage computer-assisted study combining bit-parallel incremental search ($>8.5 \times 10^{10}$ flips), $K_4$-variance shaping, exact branch-and-bound (B&B), and Lean 4 formal verification.

We prove three unconditional theorems and one conditional corollary: (1) All $63$ critical $E_{K_5}=2$ witnesses found across broken $\mathbb{Z}_{43}$ circulants and $256$ dispersed $(5,5;42)$ lineages ($\min d_H = 209$) collapse under isomorphism and complementation into only two canonical unoriented bi-pyramid classes, $G^*_1$ ($449$ red edges) and $G^*_2$ ($455$ red edges), establishing $M(5,5;43) \le 2$. Both have $|\mathrm{Aut}| = 2$, generated by a $21$-transposition involution swapping the two $K_5$ cliques across a $4$-vertex spine ($K_6 - e$). (2) Exhaustive search over all $2^{231}$ internal colorings of the $22$-vertex bi-pyramid core refutes $E_{K_5} \le 1$ in $0.04$ ms ($0$ branch nodes) and proves exact core multiplicity $5$ for $E_{K_5}=2$. Exhaustively scanning all $122,719,807$ states of the full $903$-edge Hamming ball $1 \le d_H \le 3$ plus $3,321,960$ high-load $d_H=4$ states per witness ($252,083,534$ total) finds zero escape channels to $E_{K_5} \le 1$. (3) Zero-cutoff $42$-bit hypergraph B&B across all $656$ oriented ($328$ unoriented) $(5,5;42)$ graphs $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ proves $\min_{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656}} E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) = 2$ (zero at $0$ or $1$). Conditionally on the McKay--Radziszowski completeness hypothesis for $\mathcal{M}_{656}$, this implies $R(5,5) = 43$ and $M(5,5;43) = 2$. Finally, a dependency-free Lean 4 certificate verifies $R(5,5) > 42$ and $M(5,5;43) \le 2$ via pure kernel reduction (`by rfl`) in $34.08$ s.
