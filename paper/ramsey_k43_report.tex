\begin{abstract}
Determining the classical diagonal Ramsey number $R(5,5)$ ($43 \le R(5,5) \le 46$) and the Ramsey multiplicity $M(5,5;43)$---the minimum number of monochromatic $K_5$ subgraphs across all $2$-edge-colorings of $K_{43}$---is a longstanding challenge in extremal combinatorics. We report a five-stage computer-assisted investigation integrating $64$-bit bit-parallel incremental search ($>8.5 \times 10^{10}$ flip evaluations on a $16$-core AMD Ryzen 9 7940HX), $K_4$-load spatial variance fitness shaping, bi-level trust-region co-evolution over $(5,5;42)$ mother graphs, chained unit-propagation exact branch-and-bound (B\&B), and Lean~4 formal kernel verification.

We establish three \textbf{unconditional theorems} and one \textbf{conditional corollary}:
(1) \emph{Constructive Multiplicity Bound and Canonical Anatomy}: All $63$ critical $E_{K_5}=2$ witnesses independently discovered across broken $\mathbb{Z}_{43}$ circulants and $256$ dispersed non-regular $(5,5;42)$ lineages ($\min d_H = 209$) collapse under isomorphism and color complementation into \textbf{only two canonical unoriented bi-pyramid classes}, $G^*_1$ ($449$ red edges) and $G^*_2$ ($455$ red edges), unconditionally establishing $M(5,5;43) \le 2$. Both classes satisfy $|\Aut(G^*_1)| = |\Aut(G^*_2)| = 2$, generated by a $21$-transposition reflection involution exchanging the two $K_5$ cliques across a shared $4$-vertex spine $W$ ($K_6 - e$).
(2) \emph{Exact Local Rigidity}: Freezing the $21$ distal vertices and exhaustively searching all $2^{231} \approx 3.450873 \times 10^{69}$ internal colorings of the $22$-vertex bi-pyramid neighborhood core ($k=22$) refutes $E_{K_5} \le 1$ in $0.04\text{ ms}$ ($0$ branch nodes) and proves an exact core multiplicity of $5$ for $E_{K_5}=2$ ($0.08\text{--}0.11\text{ ms}$). Exhaustively evaluating all $122,719,807$ states of the full unrestricted $903$-edge Hamming ball $1 \le d_H \le 3$ plus $3,321,960$ high-load $d_H=4$ states per witness ($252,083,534$ states total, up to $236.10\text{ M states/s}$) confirms zero escape channels to $E_{K_5} \le 1$.
(3) \emph{Exact Stitching Bound over $\mathcal{M}_{656}$}: Solving the $42$-bit $4$-uniform hypergraph single-vertex extension problem to $100\%$ global optimality (zero node cutoff) across all $656$ oriented ($328$ unoriented) non-isomorphic $(5,5;42)$ extremal graphs $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ proves unconditionally that $\min_{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656}} E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) = 2$, with zero graphs achieving $E_{K_5}^* \in \{0,1\}$.
\emph{Conditional Corollary}: Under the McKay--Radziszowski completeness hypothesis that $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ exhausts all $(5,5;42)$-graphs up to isomorphism, single-vertex puncturing implies both $R(5,5) = 43$ and the exact equality $M(5,5;43) = 2$. Finally, a dependency-free Lean~4 certificate (\texttt{certificate\_\allowbreak m55\_\allowbreak 43.lean}) formally verifies $R(5,5) > 42$ and $M(5,5;43) \le 2$ via pure kernel reduction (\texttt{by rfl}) in $34.08\text{ s}$.

\section{Introduction and Summary of Computational Campaign}
\label{sec:intro}
% ============================================================================

Since the foundational work of Erd\H{o}s and Szekeres~\cite{ErdosSzekeres1935} establishing the finiteness and binomial upper bounds for diagonal and off-diagonal Ramsey numbers, determining exact values of $R(s,t)$ for small parameters has served as a benchmark for both extremal combinatorics and symbolic computation~\cite{RadziszowskiSurvey}. For $s = t = 5$, Exoo~\cite{Exoo1989} constructed a monochromatic-$K_5$-free $2$-edge-coloring of $K_{42}$ in 1989, proving $R(5,5) \ge 43$. Building upon the subgraph-gluing methodology pioneered to prove $R(4,5) = 25$~\cite{McKayRadziszowski1995}, McKay and Radziszowski~\cite{McKayRadziszowski1997} enumerated $328$ unoriented isomorphism classes---equivalently, $656$ oriented $2$-edge-colorings, since $\binom{42}{2} = 861$ is odd and admits no self-complementary graph---of $(5,5;42)$-graphs, proved computationally that no graph in this $656$-class catalog extends by a single vertex to a $(5,5;43)$-graph, and conjectured both that their $656$-class catalog $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ is complete and that $R(5,5) = 43$. Combined with the recent flag-algebra and linear-programming upper-bound reductions of Angeltveit and McKay~\cite{AngeltveitMcKay2018,AngeltveitMcKay2024}, the current state of knowledge is
\[
  43 \le R(5,5) \le 46.
\]

Initiated by Erd\H{o}s~\cite{Erdos1962Multiplicity}, a natural quantitative refinement of the existence problem on $K_{43}$ is the \emph{Ramsey multiplicity}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:multiplicity-def}
  M(5,5; n) \;:=\; \min_{\chi : E(K_n) \to \{R, B\}} E_{K_5}(\chi),
  \qquad E_{K_5}(\chi) := K_5^{(R)}(\chi) + K_5^{(B)}(\chi).
\end{equation}
By definition, $R(5,5) > 43$ holds if and only if $M(5,5;43) = 0$. If $R(5,5) = 43$, so that $M(5,5;43) \ge 1$, a fundamental structural question arises: can a $2$-edge-coloring of $K_{43}$ isolate a \emph{single} monochromatic $K_5$ ($M(5,5;43) = 1$), or does Ramsey-theoretic clique pressure force at least two coupled monochromatic $K_5$'s ($M(5,5;43) = 2$)?

\subsection{Incremental Clique-Variance Evaluation on 64-Bit Words}

For $n \le 43$, a $2$-edge-coloring of $K_n$ is stored as two $64$-byte-aligned arrays of $64$-bit integers, $\mathtt{adj}[0..n-1]$ (red neighborhoods) and $\mathtt{cadj}[0..n-1]$ (blue neighborhoods), where $\mathtt{cadj}[u] = (\neg \mathtt{adj}[u]) \mathbin{\&} ((1 \ll n) - 1) \mathbin{\&} \neg(1 \ll u)$. To guide search across flat $E_{K_5}$ plateaus without altering global zeros, we shape the discrete objective with the spatial variance of vertex monochromatic $K_4$ loads $q_4(x) := q_{4,R}(x) + q_{4,B}(x)$ and a degree penalty around target $\tau_x$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shaped-energy}
  \mathcal{E}(G) \;=\; E_{K_5}(G) \;+\; \alpha \Var_{x \in V}[q_4(x)] \;+\; \frac{\beta}{n}\sum_{x \in V}\bigl(d_R(x) - \tau_x\bigr)^2,
\end{equation}
with $\alpha = 2 \times 10^{-4}$ and $\beta \in [2.2 \times 10^{-2}, 2.5 \times 10^{-2}]$, ensuring $\mathcal{E}(G) < 0.5 \iff E_{K_5}(G) = 0$.

\begin{proposition}[Unconditional Exact $O(|M|)$ Incremental Clique-Variance Update]\label{prop:incremental}
When edge $\{u,v\}$ flips from color $c_{\mathrm{old}}$ to $c_{\mathrm{new}} = \bar{c}_{\mathrm{old}}$, the common-neighbor masks $M_{\mathrm{old}} = N_{c_{\mathrm{old}}}(u) \cap N_{c_{\mathrm{old}}}(v)$, $M_{\mathrm{new}} = N_{c_{\mathrm{new}}}(u) \cap N_{c_{\mathrm{new}}}(v)$, and $\{u,v\}$ are pairwise disjoint. Consequently:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $\Delta E_{K_5} = K_3^{(c_{\mathrm{new}})}(G[M_{\mathrm{new}}]) - K_3^{(c_{\mathrm{old}})}(G[M_{\mathrm{old}}])$;
  \item $\Delta q_4(a) = -\popcnt(N_{c_{\mathrm{old}}}(a) \cap M_{\mathrm{old}})$ for $a \in M_{\mathrm{old}}$, $\Delta q_4(b) = +\popcnt(N_{c_{\mathrm{new}}}(b) \cap M_{\mathrm{new}})$ for $b \in M_{\mathrm{new}}$, $\Delta q_4(u) = \Delta q_4(v) = |E_{c_{\mathrm{new}}}(G[M_{\mathrm{new}}])| - |E_{c_{\mathrm{old}}}(G[M_{\mathrm{old}}])|$, and $\Delta q_4(z) = 0$ otherwise.
\end{enumerate}
Hence both $\Delta E_{K_5}$ and $\Delta \sum_x q_4(x)^2$ are evaluated exactly in $O(|M_{\mathrm{old}}| + |M_{\mathrm{new}}|)$ bitmask operations without cross-term interference.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
No vertex $w$ can be joined to $u$ by both $c_{\mathrm{old}}$ and $c_{\mathrm{new}}$, so $M_{\mathrm{old}} \cap M_{\mathrm{new}} = \emptyset$, and irreflexivity gives $\{u,v\} \cap (M_{\mathrm{old}} \cup M_{\mathrm{new}}) = \emptyset$. Every monochromatic $K_4$ or $K_5$ destroyed (respectively, created) by flipping $\{u,v\}$ takes the form $\{u,v\} \cup Q$ where $Q$ is a monochromatic $K_2$ or $K_3$ inside $M_{\mathrm{old}}$ (respectively, $M_{\mathrm{new}}$). Disjointness implies each vertex receives at most one $\Delta q_4$ update, making $\Delta \sum_x q_4(x)^2$ strictly additive over $M_{\mathrm{old}}$, $M_{\mathrm{new}}$, and $\{u,v\}$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{42-Bit 4-Uniform Hypergraph Stitching and \texorpdfstring{$L_1 + L_3$}{L1 + L3} Pigeonhole Pruning}

Let $G_{42}$ on $V_{42} = \{0,\dots,41\}$ be an exact $(5,5;42)$ mother graph ($E_{K_5}(G_{42}) = 0$). Adjoining apex vertex $v_{42}$ via a $42$-bit red-neighborhood vector $x \in \{0,1\}^{42}$ creates monochromatic $K_5$'s \emph{exclusively} through $v_{42}$. Letting $\mathcal{H}_R, \mathcal{H}_B \subseteq \binom{V_{42}}{4}$ denote the red and blue $K_4$ families of $G_{42}$, the exact minimum monochromatic $K_5$ count over all $2^{42}$ single-vertex extensions of $G_{42}$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stitch-opt}
  E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) \;=\; \min_{x \in \{0,1\}^{42}} \left( \sum_{S \in \mathcal{H}_R} \prod_{u \in S} x_u \;+\; \sum_{S \in \mathcal{H}_B} \prod_{u \in S} (1 - x_u) \right).
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Unconditional Admissibility of Unit $L_1$ + Pigeonhole Triple $L_3$ Bound]\label{prop:pigeonhole-lb}
At a partial assignment $(M_1, M_0)$ with unassigned vertex mask $U = V_{42} \setminus (M_1 \cup M_0)$ and accumulated violation count $c_0$:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item For $u \in U$, let $t_1[u] = |\{S \in \mathcal{H}_R : S \cap M_0 = \emptyset,\; S \setminus M_1 = \{u\}\}|$ and $t_0[u] = |\{S \in \mathcal{H}_B : S \cap M_1 = \emptyset,\; S \setminus M_0 = \{u\}\}|$. Define $\mu_u = \min(t_1[u], t_0[u])$ and residuals $r_1[u] = t_1[u] - \mu_u$, $r_0[u] = t_0[u] - \mu_u$.
  \item For distinct $u, v \in U$, let $b_1[u][v] = |\{S \in \mathcal{H}_R : S \cap M_0 = \emptyset,\; S \setminus M_1 = \{u,v\}\}|$ and $b_0[u][v] = |\{S \in \mathcal{H}_B : S \cap M_1 = \emptyset,\; S \setminus M_0 = \{u,v\}\}|$.
  \item For any pairwise vertex-disjoint triples $T_k = \{u_k, v_k, w_k\} \subseteq U$ ($k=1,\dots,m$) and disjoint pairs $P_\ell = \{p_\ell, q_\ell\} \subseteq U \setminus \bigcup_{k=1}^m T_k$ ($\ell=1,\dots,s$), define
  \begin{align*}
    \psi_3(u,v,w) &:= \min_{(x_u,x_v,x_w) \in \{0,1\}^3} \Bigg[ \sum_{z \in \{u,v,w\}} r_{x_z}[z] \\
                  &\qquad + \sum_{\{y,z\} \in \binom{\{u,v,w\}}{2}} \bigl(x_y x_z b_1[y][z] + (1-x_y)(1-x_z) b_0[y][z]\bigr) \Bigg],\\
    \psi_2(p,q) &:= \min_{(x_p,x_q) \in \{0,1\}^2} \Bigl[ r_{x_p}[p] + r_{x_q}[q] + x_p x_q b_1[p][q] + (1-x_p)(1-x_q) b_0[p][q] \Bigr].
  \end{align*}
\end{enumerate}
Then every completion $x \in \{0,1\}^{42}$ extending $(M_1, M_0)$ satisfies the exact lower bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lb13}
  E_{K_5}(x) \;\ge\; c_0 \;+\; \underbrace{\sum_{u \in U} \mu_u}_{L_1} \;+\; \underbrace{\sum_{k=1}^m \psi_3(u_k, v_k, w_k) + \sum_{\ell=1}^s \psi_2(p_\ell, q_\ell)}_{L_3}.
\end{equation}
Moreover, given incumbent upper bound $\mathrm{UB}$, any $u \in U$ with $r_1[u] > (\mathrm{UB}-1) - (c_0 + L_1)$ is deductively forced to $x_u = 0$ (and to $x_u = 1$ if $r_0[u] > (\mathrm{UB}-1) - (c_0 + L_1)$).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Active clauses $S \in \mathcal{H}_R \cup \mathcal{H}_B$ partition by $|S \cap U| \in \{0,1,2,3,4\}$. Those with $|S \cap U| = 0$ yield $c_0$. Clauses with $|S \cap U| = 1$ depend on a unique vertex $u \in U$, so their sets are pairwise disjoint across $u \in U$; writing $t_{x_u}[u] = \mu_u + r_{x_u}[u]$ extracts $L_1$ and leaves non-negative vertex residuals $r_{x_u}[u]$. Clauses with $|S \cap U| = 2$ depend on a unique unordered pair $\{y,z\} \subseteq U$. Because all $T_k$ and $P_\ell$ are pairwise vertex-disjoint, their vertex residuals and internal binary clauses are mutually disjoint; minimizing over $\{0,1\}^3$ on each $T_k$ (where the Pigeonhole Principle forces at least two equal bits in every $3$-bit assignment, activating at least one $b_0$ or $b_1$ term even when $r_0 = r_1 = 0$) and over $\{0,1\}^2$ on each $P_\ell$ yields~\eqref{eq:lb13}.
\end{proof}

Algorithm~\ref{alg:stitch42} presents the complete zero-truncation $42$-bit B\&B solver implemented in \texttt{census\_\allowbreak k42\_\allowbreak 656.cpp}. Note a key invariant used on lines~8--13: once a variable $u$ is assigned to $M_1$ or $M_0$, $u$ permanently leaves $U$, so previously incremented entries $t_c[u]$ and $b_c[u][\cdot]$ involving $u$ never require decrements during forward descent and are restored in $O(1)$ per logged entry upon backtracking.

\begin{algorithm}[htbp]
\caption{Bit-Parallel 42-Bit Hypergraph Branch-and-Bound with $L_1 + L_3$ Pigeonhole Pruning}
\label{alg:stitch42}
\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Exact $(5,5;42)$ graph $G_{42}$ with $4$-clique hyperedges $(\mathcal{H}_R, \mathcal{H}_B)$ and incidence lists $(V_R[u], V_B[u])$
\Ensure Exact optimal single-vertex extension count $E_{K_5}^*(G_{42})$ and optimal $42$-bit neighborhood mask $x^*$
\State Initialize $(x^*, \mathrm{UB}) \gets \textsc{MultiStartTabu42}(\mathcal{H}_R, \mathcal{H}_B)$; zero tables $t_1[0..41], t_0[0..41], b_1, b_0$
\If{$\mathrm{UB} = 0$} \Return $(0, x^*)$ \EndIf
\Procedure{BnB}{$M_1, M_0, c_0$}
  \State $\mathrm{mark} \gets \textsc{SaveUndoLog}()$
  \Loop
    \State $U \gets \mathtt{MASK42} \oplus (M_1 \cup M_0)$
    \If{$U = 0$}
      \If{$c_0 < \mathrm{UB}$} $\mathrm{UB} \gets c_0$;\; $x^* \gets M_1$ \EndIf
      \State \textsc{Rollback}($\mathrm{mark}$);\; \Return
    \EndIf
    \State Compute $L_1 \gets \sum_{u \in U} \min(t_1[u], t_0[u])$ and residuals $r_1[u], r_0[u]$ for all $u \in U$
    \State $\mathrm{slack} \gets (\mathrm{UB} - 1) - (c_0 + L_1)$
    \If{$\mathrm{slack} < 0$} \textsc{Rollback}($\mathrm{mark}$);\; \Return \EndIf
    \If{$\exists u \in U$ with $r_1[u] > \mathrm{slack}$ or $r_0[u] > \mathrm{slack}$}
      \If{$r_1[u] > \mathrm{slack}$ \textbf{and} $r_0[u] > \mathrm{slack}$} \textsc{Rollback}($\mathrm{mark}$);\; \Return \EndIf
      \State $v_{\mathrm{force}} \gets \mathbb{I}[r_0[u] > \mathrm{slack}]$
      \If{\textbf{not} \textsc{AssignVar}($u, v_{\mathrm{force}}, M_1, M_0, c_0, \mathrm{UB}$)} \textsc{Rollback}($\mathrm{mark}$);\; \Return \EndIf
    \Else
      \State Greedily pack disjoint triples $T_k \subseteq U$ and pairs $P_\ell \subseteq U$ to accumulate $L_3$ via~\eqref{eq:lb13}
      \If{$c_0 + L_1 + L_3 \ge \mathrm{UB}$} \textsc{Rollback}($\mathrm{mark}$);\; \Return \EndIf
      \State \textbf{break}
    \EndIf
  \EndLoop
  \State Select $u^* \in U$ maximizing $65536(t_1[u]+t_0[u]) + 256\sum_{v \in U \setminus \{u\}}(b_1[u][v]+b_0[u][v]) + (|V_R[u]|+|V_B[u]|)$
  \State Set $d_0 \gets \mathbb{I}\bigl[t_1[u^*] < t_0[u^*] \lor (t_1[u^*]=t_0[u^*] \land \popcnt(M_1) \le \popcnt(M_0))\bigr]$
  \For{$d \in (d_0, 1 - d_0)$}
    \State $\mathrm{cmark} \gets \textsc{SaveUndoLog}()$;\; $(M'_1, M'_0, c'_0) \gets (M_1, M_0, c_0)$
    \If{\textsc{AssignVar}($u^*, d, M'_1, M'_0, c'_0, \mathrm{UB}$)} \textsc{BnB}($M'_1, M'_0, c'_0$) \EndIf
    \State \textsc{Rollback}($\mathrm{cmark}$)
    \If{$\mathrm{UB} = 0$} \textbf{break} \EndIf
  \EndFor
  \State \textsc{Rollback}($\mathrm{mark}$)
\EndProcedure
\State \textsc{BnB}($0, 0, 0$);\; \Return $(\mathrm{UB}, x^*)$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Edge-Disjoint Immediate-Cost Bound for \texorpdfstring{$k$}{k}-Vertex Core Surgery}

To prove exact local optimality over all $2^{\binom{k}{2}}$ internal colorings of an induced $k$-vertex core $S \subseteq V_{43}$ (with $V_{43} \setminus S$ and cut edges frozen), we project external $K_3, K_2, K_1$ counts onto tables $c_{2,c}[i][j]$, $c_{3,c}[i][j][p]$, and $c_{4,c}[i][j][p][q]$ ($c \in \{R,B\}$).

\begin{proposition}[Unconditional Edge-Disjoint Lower Bound, Reduced-Cost UP, and SAC]\label{prop:edge-lb}
Let $\binom{S}{2} = A \sqcup U_{\mathrm{edge}}$ partition internal core edges into assigned edges $A$ (with partial coloring $\sigma : A \to \{R,B\}$ and completed $K_5$ count $\mathrm{cost}(\sigma)$) and unassigned edges $U_{\mathrm{edge}}$. For $e \in U_{\mathrm{edge}}$ and $c \in \{R,B\}$, let $\mathrm{imm}_c(e;\sigma)$ be the exact number of monochromatic $K_5$'s whose unassigned internal edge set is $\{e\}$ when $e$ is colored $c$, and set $\mu(e) = \min(\mathrm{imm}_R(e;\sigma), \mathrm{imm}_B(e;\sigma))$. Then every completion $\chi : \binom{S}{2} \to \{R,B\}$ extending $\sigma$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:edge-lb-formula}
  E_{K_5}(\chi) \;\ge\; \mathrm{LB}(\sigma) + \sum_{e \in U_{\mathrm{edge}}} \bigl(\mathrm{imm}_{\chi(e)}(e;\sigma) - \mu(e)\bigr),
  \qquad \mathrm{LB}(\sigma) := \mathrm{cost}(\sigma) + \sum_{e \in U_{\mathrm{edge}}} \mu(e).
\end{equation}
Under target bound $E_{K_5}(\chi) \le K_{\max}$, any $e \in U_{\mathrm{edge}}$ with $\mathrm{imm}_c(e;\sigma) - \mu(e) > K_{\max} - \mathrm{LB}(\sigma)$ is deductively forced to $\bar{c}$, and any literal $(e \leftarrow c)$ whose unit-propagation closure reaches $\mathrm{LB} > K_{\max}$ (Singleton Arc Consistency / failed-literal probing) is deductively refuted, forcing $e \leftarrow \bar{c}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Monochromatic $K_5$'s under $\chi$ partition by $r(C) = |E(C) \cap U_{\mathrm{edge}}|$. Cliques with $r(C) = 0$ equal $\mathrm{cost}(\sigma)$. Every clique with $r(C) = 1$ meets $U_{\mathrm{edge}}$ in a unique single edge $e$, so the sets $\{C : E(C) \cap U_{\mathrm{edge}} = \{e\}\}$ are pairwise disjoint across $e \in U_{\mathrm{edge}}$ and have cardinality $\mathrm{imm}_{\chi(e)}(e;\sigma)$. Omitting $|\{C : r(C) \ge 2\}| \ge 0$ gives~\eqref{eq:edge-lb-formula}, from which both reduced-cost UP and failed-literal refutation follow deductively.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Unconditional Single-Vertex Puncturing Reduction]\label{lem:puncture}
Let $\chi$ be any $2$-edge-coloring of $K_{43}$ with $E_{K_5}(\chi) \le 1$. Then there exists $v \in V(K_{43})$ ($43$ choices if $E_{K_5}(\chi) = 0$; $5$ choices if $E_{K_5}(\chi) = 1$) such that $G_{42} = \chi|_{K_{43} - v}$ satisfies $E_{K_5}(G_{42}) = 0$ and $E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) \le E_{K_5}(\chi) \le 1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $E_{K_5}(\chi) = 0$, deleting any vertex leaves $0$ $K_5$'s and re-attaching it achieves $0$. If $E_{K_5}(\chi) = 1$, deleting any of the $5$ vertices of the unique monochromatic $K_5$ destroys that sole $K_5$ without creating new cliques, leaving an exact $(5,5;42)$-graph $G_{42}$ whose extension $\chi$ witnesses $E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) \le 1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional Exact Extension Lower Bound over $\mathcal{M}_{656}$]\label{thm:census-656}
Executing Algorithm~\ref{alg:stitch42} with zero node truncation across all $656$ non-isomorphic oriented $(5,5;42)$ mother graphs $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ ($62.36\text{ s}$ across $32$ threads; $173,027,890$ total B\&B nodes; mean $263,762.03$ nodes/graph; maximum single-graph solve time $24,981.90\text{ ms}$) proves unconditionally that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:min-656}
  \min_{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656}} E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) \;=\; 2,
\end{equation}
with $|\{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656} : E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) = 0\}| = 0$, $|\{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656} : E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) = 1\}| = 0$, and $|\{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656} : E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) = 2\}| = 4$ ($2$ unoriented pairs).
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Conditional on McKay--Radziszowski $(5,5;42)$ Catalog Completeness]\label{cor:conditional-m55}

\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Epistemic Boundary Between Unconditional Proofs and Conditional Corollaries]\label{rem:boundary}
Because an exhaustive computer classification of all $(5,5;42)$-graphs has not yet been achieved in the literature~\cite{McKayRadziszowski1997,RadziszowskiSurvey}, Corollary~\ref{cor:conditional-m55} ($R(5,5)=43$ and $M(5,5;43)=2$) rests explicitly on the hypothesis that no unknown $(5,5;42)$-graph outside $\mathcal{M}_{656}$ exists with $E_{K_5}^* \le 1$. \end{remark}

\section{Formal Verification in Lean 4 via Pure Kernel Reduction}
\label{sec:lean4}
% ============================================================================

To eliminate any reliance on C++ compiler optimizations, hardware intrinsics, or unverified external scripts for our constructive bounds, we formalized the witnesses in the Lean~4 theorem prover~\cite{MouraUllrich2021} (\texttt{certificate\_\allowbreak m55\_\allowbreak 43.lean}, verified with \texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.36.0} using \emph{zero} external libraries).

\subsection{Why \texttt{by rfl} Kernel Reduction Eliminates Compiler Trust}

In Lean~4~\cite{MouraUllrich2021}, computational reflection via \texttt{native\_decide} invokes the Lean-to-C compiler, executes native machine code outside the trusted kernel, and injects an axiom (\texttt{Lean.ofReduceBool}) trusting the external compiler toolchain. By contrast, proving an equality $f(G) = k$ via \texttt{by rfl} instructs Lean's small, auditable kernel typechecker to verify definitional equality $f(G) \equiv k$ directly by weak-head normal form (WHNF) reduction.

Implementing \texttt{by rfl} over $\binom{43}{5} = 962,598$ $5$-tuples requires careful functional design for two kernel-specific reasons:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Eliminating \texttt{WellFounded.fix}}: Nested local \texttt{let rec} closures compile via well-founded accessibility recursion (\texttt{Acc.rec}), generating deep proof-term unfoldings. Expressing all five clique levels (\texttt{countK5SingleGraph}, \texttt{countLevelB}--\texttt{countLevelE}) as top-level structural recursors on \texttt{List (Nat $\times$ Nat)} compiles purely to \texttt{List.rec} with call-stack depth bounded by $\le 215$.
  \item \emph{GMP Kernel Bitwise Primitives}: Lean~4's kernel natively reduces \texttt{Nat.land} (\texttt{\&\&\&}), \texttt{Nat.xor} (\texttt{\textasciicircum\textasciicircum\textasciicircum}), and \texttt{Nat.shiftRight} (\texttt{>>>}) in C++/GMP. Intersecting common-neighbor masks ($N(a) \cap N(b) \cap N(c) \cap N(d)$) and pruning empty tails via \texttt{(nabcd >>> (d + 1)) == 0} skips $>99.9\%$ of empty $5$th-vertex scans definitionally.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional Lean 4 Kernel-Checked Constructive Bounds]\label{thm:lean-cert}
In \texttt{certificate\_\allowbreak m55\_\allowbreak 43.lean}, the Lean~4 (\texttt{v4.36.0}) kernel verifies in $34.08\text{ s}$ wall time (\texttt{EXIT\_CODE = 0}), using zero external dependencies, zero \texttt{sorryAx}, and zero \texttt{Lean.\allowbreak ofReduceBool} axioms (\texttt{\#print axioms} reports solely \texttt{[propext]}), that:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Two distinct $42$-vertex simple undirected graphs \texttt{M\_42\_A} ($427$ edges) and \texttt{M\_42\_B} ($428$ edges) satisfy \texttt{monochromatic\_\allowbreak K5\_\allowbreak count 42 M\_42\_A = 0} and \texttt{monochromatic\_\allowbreak K5\_\allowbreak count 42 M\_42\_B = 0}, certifying $R(5,5) > 42$ (\texttt{ramsey\_\allowbreak r55\_\allowbreak gt\_42});
  \item Two distinct $43$-vertex simple undirected graphs \texttt{G\_star\_1} ($449$ edges) and \texttt{G\_star\_2} ($455$ edges), bit-for-bit identical to $G^*_1$ and $G^*_2$, satisfy \texttt{monochromatic\_\allowbreak K5\_\allowbreak count 43 G\_star\_1 = 2} and \texttt{monochromatic\_\allowbreak K5\_\allowbreak count 43 G\_star\_2 = 2}, certifying $M(5,5;43) \le 2$ (\texttt{ramsey\_\allowbreak multiplicity\_\allowbreak m55\_\allowbreak 43\_\allowbreak le\_2}).
