Dear Editors,

Please accept for consideration our manuscript *"Critical Bi-Pyramid Rigidity, Exact 42-Bit Hypergraph Stitching over the 656 $(5,5;42)$ Catalog, and Formal Certification of $M(5,5;43) \le 2$"* as an original research article.

Determining the classical diagonal Ramsey number $R(5,5)$ ($43 \le R(5,5) \le 46$, Exoo 1989; McKay--Radziszowski 1997; Angeltveit--McKay 2024) and characterizing the Ramsey multiplicity $M(5,5;43)$---the minimum number of monochromatic $K_5$ subgraphs across all $2$-edge-colorings of $K_{43}$---are central problems at the interface of extremal combinatorics and symbolic computation. Even under the longstanding conjecture $R(5,5) = 43$ ($M(5,5;43) \ge 1$), it was not previously resolved whether a $2$-edge-coloring of $K_{43}$ can isolate a single monochromatic $5$-clique ($M(5,5;43) = 1$) or whether topological clique coupling forces $M(5,5;43) = 2$.

Combining $>8.5 \times 10^{10}$ bit-parallel incremental evaluations, exact branch-and-bound with deductive pruning, and formal theorem proving, our paper makes three principal contributions:

1. **Unconditional Constructive Multiplicity Bound $M(5,5;43) \le 2$ and Canonical Bi-Pyramid Classification (Theorem 3.1):**  
   Across $63$ critical $E_{K_5}=2$ witnesses independently obtained from symmetry-broken $\mathbb{Z}_{43}$ circulants and $256$ strongly dispersed non-regular $(5,5;42)$ lineages ($\min d_H = 209$), exact $S_{43}$ isomorphism testing collapses all solutions into **only two canonical unoriented critical bi-pyramid classes**, $G^*_1$ ($449$ red edges) and $G^*_2$ ($455$ red edges). We prove that both satisfy $|\operatorname{Aut}(G^*_1)| = |\operatorname{Aut}(G^*_2)| = 2$, generated by an explicit $21$-transposition reflection involution that exchanges the two monochromatic $K_5$ cliques across a shared $4$-vertex spine $W$ ($K_6 - e$), and that their $8$ spine-punctured $(5,5;42)$ subgraphs ($|\operatorname{Aut}| = 1$) collapse via color-complementation duality into just two unoriented mother classes.

2. **Exact Local Rigidity Proofs and Zero-Truncation 656-Class $(5,5;42)$ Stitching Census (Theorems 3.2 & 4.2, Corollary 4.3):**  
   - *Local Rigidity:* Using an exact incremental edge-disjoint lower bound coupled with chained Unit Propagation and Singleton Arc Consistency (SAC), we exhaustively search all $2^{231} \approx 3.45 \times 10^{69}$ colorings of the $22$-vertex bi-pyramid neighborhood core ($k=22$, distal $21$ vertices frozen), refuting $E_{K_5} \le 1$ in $0.04\text{ ms}$ ($0$ branch nodes) and proving an exact core multiplicity of $5$ for $E_{K_5}=2$. Simultaneously, an exhaustive scan of all $122,719,807$ states in the full unrestricted $903$-edge Hamming ball $1 \le d_H \le 3$ (plus $3,321,960$ high-load $d_H=4$ states per witness) confirms zero escape channels to $E_{K_5} \le 1$.
   - *Global Census over $\mathcal{M}_{656}$:* Introducing an admissible vertex-disjoint Pigeonhole Triple lower bound ($L_1 + L_3$, Algorithm 1), we solve the $42$-bit $4$-uniform hypergraph single-vertex extension problem to $100\%$ global optimality (zero node cutoff) across all $656$ non-isomorphic oriented ($328$ unoriented) $(5,5;42)$ extremal graphs $\mathcal{M}_{656}$, proving unconditionally that $\min_{G_{42} \in \mathcal{M}_{656}} E_{K_5}^*(G_{42}) = 2$ (with zero classes achieving $E_{K_5}^* \in \{0,1\}$). Careful attention is paid to separating unconditional theorems from conditional deductions: as stated explicitly in Corollary 4.3 and Remark 4.4, under the McKay--Radziszowski completeness hypothesis for $\mathcal{M}_{656}$, single-vertex puncturing implies both $R(5,5) = 43$ and the exact equality $M(5,5;43) = 2$.
